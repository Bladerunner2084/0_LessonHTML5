Your story stays yours.

Writeline assists the author. The author owns the story. That isn't a slogan — it's a field on every record.

🟢 CanonYou established it.
🟡 ProvisionalA working assumption, not settled.
🔵 AI suggestionProposed. Not yours until you say so.
🔴 Non-canonConsidered and rejected. Kept as a record.

Anything you create is canon by default. Only the AI layer may propose, and only you may promote. Drafted prose resting on an unsettled fact is flagged; resting on a rejected one is an error. A locked decision cannot be silently worked around. A system that can't tell a machine's guess from an author's decision will eventually launder one into the other — preventing exactly that is the point.

Develop your own literary voice.

You never type an author's name. You paste prose that sounds like what you're aiming at, and Writeline measures it — sentence rhythm, dialogue ratio, adverb and filter-word rates, punctuation fingerprint. It keeps the numbers and discards the text.

One story is powerful. An entire universe is something else.

Characters, locations, organisations, world rules, history, unresolved threads — shared across books, stored once. Edit a character in book one and book four already knows.

Shared canon lives at the project level; each book keeps its own chapters, scenes, versions and audits. No book can overwrite another's story.
